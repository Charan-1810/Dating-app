# Product Brief (working name: TBD)

> **A dating app for Gen Z that rewards intention, not swiping.**
> Launched first to eLitmus's 1M+ verified users (2020–2023 batches), then opened to everyone 18+.

---

## 1. Problem
- **Swipe fatigue.** Hundreds of swipes, few matches, and chats that die after "hi".
- **People feel replaceable.** The swipe "buffet" makes everyone seem disposable, because someone better always seems to be next.
- **Trust and safety.** Fake profiles, catfishing and dead profiles. In India especially, women get flooded with low-effort messages, leave the app, and the gender ratio gets worse.
- **Shallow profiles.** Short bio limits and coached profiles show a persona, not the real person.
- **Nothing happens offline.** Matches rarely turn into actual meetings.

## 2. User research (interviews, Nov 2026)
We spoke with three dating-app users, one of them a user for 13+ years.

| What we heard | What we changed |
|---|---|
| "Swiping forces you to decide before seeing your options or reading bios. Bring back search." | **Search and browse with filters** replaces the swipe stack and the Daily Drop |
| "The buffet makes people expendable." | **5 likes per day**: browse freely, but choose carefully |
| "Short bio limits serve no purpose. Fakes are short and generic." | **No character limits** on prompt answers |
| "Coached profiles are a catfish. I want to know how someone actually thinks." | **Scenario questions** generate a personality trait summary |
| "Dead profiles are one of the most broken things." | **Auto-pause after 14 days inactive**, plus an "Active" badge |
| "Let past dates leave opt-in, moderated reviews." | **Date reviews** on the roadmap |
| "Both should pay. Partner with local businesses." | **Venue partnerships** in the business model |
| "Why exclude anyone? Use filters instead." | eLitmus is the **verified launch group**, not a limit; open to all 18+ later, with an age-range filter |

**Our own call:** we considered hiding photos until people had chatted. We dropped it, because attraction matters and hiding photos wastes people's time. Photos are **visible from the start, shown alongside answers**.

## 3. Competitor landscape

| App | Model | Major flaws |
|---|---|---|
| **Tinder** (Match Group) | Swipe | Declining Gen Z use; known as a hookup app; expensive paid tiers that put matches behind a paywall; bots and fakes |
| **Bumble** | Swipe, women message first | Layoffs and user decline (2024–25); dropped its "women message first" rule; chats die after the first message |
| **Hinge** (Match Group) | Prompts, "designed to be deleted" | Charges for better visibility (HingeX); limited free likes; shows mostly the most popular profiles |
| **OkCupid** (Match Group) | Questions and match % | Lost its search-based depth; moved toward swiping and paywalls |
| **Facebook Dating** | Free, swipe | Low engagement; privacy concerns |
| **Coffee Meets Bagel** | Few curated matches a day | Small user base; matches feel random |
| **Aisle** (India) | Serious dating, credits | Paid messaging; small user base outside metro cities |
| **TrulyMadly / QuackQuack** (India) | Swipe | Fake profiles; skewed gender ratio; low-effort messages to women |

**Problems across the industry:** revenue comes from people staying on the app, not finding someone (the US FTC sued Match Group over deceptive practices; it settled in 2025). Other problems: swipe burnout, gender imbalance, romance scams, paywalls on basic features, and few matches turning into real dates.

## 4. Insight (the core argument)
Swipe apps make money when you **keep swiping**, not when you **find someone**.

**Our principle:** every feature must reward *intention*: better, safer connections that lead to meeting in real life.

## 5. Solution: how it works

| # | Feature | Why (traces back to the problem) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Verified sign-up.** eLitmus users get a "Verified" badge. 18+ only. | Trust from day 1; no fake profiles |
| 2 | **Personality questions.** About 10 scenario questions ("Your friend cancels last minute — you…") generate an OCEAN-style trait summary. | Shows how someone actually thinks, not a coached persona |
| 3 | **Prompts with no character limit** | Real people write real detail; fakes stand out |
| 4 | **Search and browse.** A profile grid with filters (age range, city, intent, interests) and keyword search across answers. | See your options before deciding; no swipe stack |
| 5 | **5 likes per day** | Choosing carefully instead of mass-liking; people aren't disposable |
| 6 | **Like with a comment.** To like someone, reply to a specific photo or answer. | Ends low-effort "hi" messages; starts conversations with real content |
| 7 | **Photos shown with answers.** Photos are visible from the start, alternating with answers and traits. Optional setting: "Show my photos only after a match". | Looks matter, and so does substance; the optional setting adds privacy |
| 8 | **Auto-pause inactive profiles.** Hidden from search after 14 days inactive; "Active this week" badge. | No dead profiles; every like reaches someone real |
| 9 | **Report and block** in one tap, always free | Safety is never paywalled |
| 10 | **Plan a Date** *(roadmap)*. Suggest a partner venue and time; share details with a trusted contact. | Moves people into real life, safely |
| 11 | **Post-date check-in** *(roadmap)*. "How did you feel about yourself with them?" | Helps people notice good signs, not just attraction |
| 12 | **Opt-in date reviews** *(roadmap)*. Moderated, and only for people who agree to be reviewed. | Trust you earn; bad actors won't opt in, and users notice |

## 6. Why us (unfair advantage)
- **No empty app at launch.** A new dating app has no users, so nobody joins. We start with 1M+ real users.
- **Pre-verified identities.** eLitmus already knows these users are real people.
- **A clear launch group.** Similar age, education and life stage (early-career, often just relocated), which makes matching easier at first. After launch it opens to everyone 18+, with filters instead of exclusion.

## 7. Business model
Free users get the full core experience, including search, 5 daily likes, messaging and safety.

**Premium (~₹199/month)** sells *depth*, not more swiping:
- "Why we matched" compatibility breakdown
- Advanced filters (e.g. by personality traits)
- Reopen a closed chat once

**Venue partnerships:** cafés and activity venues pay to be suggested in Plan a Date. Couples who meet there scan a code and get premium credit, so the app earns money when people actually meet.

**Never paid:** safety, verification, messaging, seeing who liked you.

## 9. Legal and compliance (India)
- **DPDP Act 2023.** eLitmus data cannot be reused without fresh, explicit consent. Users opt in, and we import only what they approve. Account deletion removes their data.
- **18+ age gate** at sign-up.
- **IT Rules 2021.** Grievance officer contact, terms of service and privacy policy published in the app.
- **Data minimisation.** No exact location stored; city only.
- **Content moderation.** Report → review queue; blocking is instant. Date reviews are moderated before they appear.

## 10. What we deliberately did NOT build (and why)
- **No swiping.** It's the problem we're solving.
- **No hidden photos by default.** Attraction matters; hiding photos wastes people's time.
- **No bio character limits.** They limit real self-expression and help fakes blend in.
- **No exact location or distance.** It's a safety risk, and city-level is enough.
- **No paid likes or boosts.** Those reward money, not intention.
- **No "max 3 open chats" rule.** Our interviews didn't support it.

## 11. How we measure success
- % of likes (with comment) that get a reply
- % of matches that turn into a real conversation (10+ messages)
- % of conversations that lead to a planned date
- % of profiles active in the last 14 days
- Women's retention after 30 days
